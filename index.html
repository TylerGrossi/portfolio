BI · Analytics · AI toolingPower BI

Golf performance analytics

Analytics on my own golf game, from round scores down to individual launch-monitor swings. The results are presented in a 22-page Power BI report and can be queried in plain language through Claude.

  • The data comes from a hand-maintained 12-sheet workbook plus five datasets derived in Python.
  • Compares my game with two other golfers across 94 rounds and 1,638 logged holes.

Models, AI & numbers

Round profilingZ-scores
Each part of the game (driving, irons, wedges, putting) is scored separately against each golfer's own baseline. Beyond ±0.5 standard deviations an area reads "on" or "off", so a round can read "driver on, putter off". The report compares average score when each area is on vs off.
Strokes gained & clutchPython
Hole-level strokes gained (off the tee, approach, putting) on simplified baselines. A clutch score compares scoring on holes 16–18 with holes 1–15, and random forest models relate putts, fairways and greens in regulation to scoring.
Club distancesLaunch monitor
Sets a carry range for each club and compares smash factor to expected, which guides club selection and practice priorities.1,734 range shots analyzed
Handicap & ball flightDAX
A point-in-time handicap index (best 8 of the last 20 differentials as of any date), and ball-flight curves drawn per club from launch angle and apex, using calculated tables and trigonometry.
Course geometryGeospatial
Downloads mapped holes, greens and hazards for 15 courses from OpenStreetMap and adds USGS lidar elevation. A tee-shot planner weighs each club's launch-monitor dispersion against bunkers, water and my measured left/right miss pattern.15 courses · 252 holes with mapped greens · 1,835 elevation points
MCP serverAI
66 analysis tools (scoring, handicap, club gapping, putting, shot shape, club fitting, charts) that let Claude answer questions about the data. Containerized and deployed to Google Cloud Run as a Claude connector. It redeploys after each logged round, with scale-to-zero and a $1 budget alert.66 tools · works in claude.ai and the Claude mobile app
LLM evaluationAI
A harness connects a local model (Qwen3 8B via Ollama) to the server as a real MCP client and scores whether it picks the right tool and arguments. Its failures exposed data bugs rather than model errors, including a handicap formula that didn't follow the World Handicap System's best-8-of-20 rule and 9-hole rounds counted as 18.tool selection: real questions 14/16 → 16/16 · scripted set 40/42 → 42/42

FBB Sim: fantasy basketball analytics

A simulation and decision platform for my 15-category ESPN fantasy basketball league. It turns live league data into win probabilities, playoff odds and recommended roster moves.

  • Live league data refreshes every 2 hours during the season (GitHub Actions) into a 35-page Next.js app.
  • About 40,000 lines of TypeScript and 11,000 lines of Python. It started as a Streamlit app and moved to Next.js with the same calculation engine.

Models, AI & numbers

Matchup modelProbabilistic
Each team's final total in each of 15 categories is modeled as a normal distribution. Variance has two parts: per-game noise and projection error. Correlated outcomes are drawn with a Gaussian copula, with an exact Poisson-binomial fallback.10,000 seeded draws · 0.17 ms per evaluation in the browser
Parameter fittingEstimation
Variances, out-of-sample projection error and a 15×15 category correlation matrix are measured from a full season of box scores, replacing hand-picked guesses.FT variance corrected 0.2 → 0.67 · FGM–PTS correlation 0.94
Season simulatorMonte Carlo
Simulates the rest of the season and the playoff bracket 10,000 times, using ESPN's injury return dates and the daily lineup cap. Each player's total is drawn in closed form instead of game by game.385 ms → 10 ms (~40× faster), identical distribution
Player valueZ-scores
Standard deviations above replacement level across 15 categories, with volume-weighted shooting percentages, so 4-of-6 counts twice as much as 2-of-3.
Roster optimizersOptimization
Streaming analysis scores every add/drop pair. The Week Planner plans a whole week of adds, drops and IR activations with greedy search plus a local-search pass under the weekly acquisition limit. On crowded days, a lineup optimizer tries every legal ESPN lineup and picks the one that wins the most expected categories.1,274 scenarios in 13.5 ms · ~10–40 ms per planner step on a 126-player pool
Injury availabilityGradient boosting
Two calibrated models predict each player's chance of playing, built from official NBA injury reports (2018–2026, parsed from PDFs) joined to box scores. Validated walk-forward by season, with a label leak caught and removed (it had inflated AUC to 0.988).game-day AUC 0.80 vs 0.72 · log loss 0.514 vs 0.554 · better in 6 of 6 seasons
AI agentLLM tool calling
A Gemini agent answers league questions in plain language. It never does the math itself. It calls tools that return the model's real numbers, then explains a recommendation grounded in them.
PerformanceEngineering
Profiling-driven fixes to the pipeline and front end, including replacing Plotly with hand-built SVG charts.API calls 7.45 s → 0.48 s · JS bundle 10.7 → 5.7 MB

A forecast log records each week's prediction before games are played, so calibration can be measured honestly over the season.

Puck Engine: NHL advanced stats

A public NHL analytics site with leaderboards, player and goalie cards, multi-year projections and an "Armchair GM" trade tool. Every advanced stat is computed in house from the NHL's raw play-by-play and shift data.

  • Replaced two third-party data providers (HockeyStats and MoneyPuck) entirely as of October 2026.
  • Built solo: 506 commits since February 2026, and first page load cut from 2.14 MB to 0.78 MB (−64%).

Models, AI & numbers

Expected goalsLightGBM
Shot model on distance, angle, shot type, preceding event, time since last event, rebounds, strength state and shift length. A single model with indicator columns beat separate per-state models on every strength state. The trees are exported to JSON and scored in plain JavaScript for live games.held-out season, 110,997 shots: AUC 0.795 · log loss 0.213 · 7,673 xG vs 7,894 goals
WARRidge / RAPM
Builds stints from shift charts and fits regularized adjusted plus-minus with sparse ridge regression, validated against established public sources.high-danger chances within 0.4% of Natural Stat Trick · all 3,006 MoneyPuck lines matched within 0.3%
ProjectionskNN + weighting
Multi-year projections of WAR, points and goals that blend weighted history, k-nearest-neighbor comparable players (k = 40, within 2 years of age) and a persistence term. 15 candidate models were compared.1,652 careers, 8,313 seasons: WAR R² 0.775 · points R² 0.727 · goals R² 0.698
Trade valueFitted model
Prices players, prospects and picks (contract, age, no-trade clauses) by minimizing value imbalance across real trades. A change is accepted only if it beats the baseline by more than 2 standard errors on paired folds.1,547 trades · held-out r = 0.74 (5-fold × 5)
ContractsRegression
Predicts contract term and cap hit, with a separate top-of-market model for elite players and a goalie-specific regression.trained on 6,363 skater + 643 goalie contracts through 2024
Prospects & playoffsOrdinal / logistic
An ordinal model of NHL outcome tiers by development season, and a logistic model of playoff games on points difference plus home ice.3,702 prospect rows (2008–17 drafts) · 866 playoff games

Movie rating predictor

A pipeline that learns my taste from 314 films I've rated and predicts my score for each of the 774 films on my watchlist. The results feed an 11-page Power BI report.

  • ETL: a Selenium export from IMDb, then enrichment through the OMDb and TMDB APIs (Rotten Tomatoes scores, cast, crew, keywords, box office), with caching, resume-after-interruption and handling of a 1,000-call daily limit.
  • Data quality: title-plus-year matching stops remakes being confused, and newly rated watchlist films move into the training set automatically.

Models, AI & numbers

Baseline modelsTree ensembles
Random forest, gradient boosting and XGBoost regressors compared with k-fold cross-validation, plus a classifier for the probability I rate a film 8 or higher.
Leakage auditValidation
Found three separate leaks. Score-based averages included each film's own score: 173 of 311 films had a one-film writer, so "writer score" was nearly the label. The fix was to recompute every score-derived feature inside each fold.original CV R² 0.803 → −0.12 once measured properly
Collaborative filteringMovieLens 32M
Matched my films to the MovieLens research dataset by IMDb ID, then added look-alike-user and item-item similarity signals, both chosen inside each CV fold.286 of 311 rated and 665 of 774 watchlist films matched · 11.0M ratings from 200,731 users
Final modelStacked ensemble
A critics baseline plus a personal-deviation model, collaborative signals, plot neighbours and genre-avoidance penalties, combined in a non-negative stack of ridge, random forest and gradient boosting.later watches: MAE 0.976 vs 1.017 critics-only · beat previous model on 5 of 5 time folds · rank correlation 0.518 vs 0.479
Plot embeddingsNLP experiment
Embedded 1,086 plots with a sentence-transformer (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) and tested three feature designs. None beat the baseline under a 2-standard-error rule, so they weren't adopted.

Earnings Momentum Model

A trading strategy that buys stocks with positive momentum (20-day SMA above 50-day, plus a Barchart buy signal) during the week they report earnings, with a −10% stop-loss and a 5-day exit. It is automated end to end, from data collection to placing paper trades.

  • About 7,400 lines of Python. 640 commits since November 2024: 237 of my own and 403 automated data updates from the pipeline.
  • The Streamlit app combines a live scanner, stop-loss analysis, EPS beat/miss analysis, an embedded Power BI report and a portfolio optimizer.

Models, AI & numbers

Data pipelineGitHub Actions
Runs twice a day (7 AM and 4 PM ET). It scrapes Finviz and Barchart for signals, validates earnings dates against yfinance, and records 1- to 90-day returns, EPS surprise, valuation fields and hourly prices.614 earnings events tracked
BacktesterHourly data
Stop-loss backtest on hourly prices that takes the actual gap loss when a stock opens below its stop, instead of assuming a perfect fill. It compares stop levels against buy-and-hold.
Win probabilityClassifier
A random forest / gradient boosting classifier predicts a positive 5-day return from P/E, beta, market cap, sector and earnings timing, and scores each new entry in the app.
Risk reportingPower BI
Compares 3-day and 5-day holds on win rate, annualized return, Sharpe ratio, weekly and annualized volatility, 95% VaR and peak capital required.
ExecutionAlpaca API
A runner reads the day's signals and places market-on-close orders at 3:55 PM ET in a paper trading account.
AI assistantGemini · MCP
A Gemini assistant in the app answers questions about the strategy data, and an MCP server exposes the same data to Claude.
